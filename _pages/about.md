I am a Postdoctoral Fellow jointly appointed by
[The Chinese University of Hong Kong (CUHK)](https://www.cuhk.edu.hk/english/) and
[The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen (CUHK-Shenzhen)](https://www.cuhk.edu.cn/en).
I received my Ph.D. in Mathematics from the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at
[Southern University of Science and Technology (SUSTech)](https://www.sustech.edu.cn/en/),
advised by Prof. [Jiang Liu](https://faculty.sustech.edu.cn/liuj/).

My research focuses on **medical image analysis**, including:

- OCT image segmentation
- Uncertainty estimation for medical image segmentation
- Semi-supervised and self-supervised learning

Before joining SUSTech, I received my M.Eng. in Software Engineering from Sichuan University
and my B.Eng. in Biomedical Engineering from Army Medical University.
